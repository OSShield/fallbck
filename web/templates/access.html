A role is a set of permissions. A binding grants a role to a user (their verified OIDC email), a group (a value of the groups claim) or a claim value (such as an app role) on the objects its selector matches; an empty selector means everything. Access is the union of all bindings, and no binding means no access. Changes apply within 30 seconds. Bindings nobody has signed in with for 90 days are marked stale.
